What can I automate?

Read down the list. Anything that makes you wince is something I can take off your plate.

  • Missed calls & messages
  • Quotes & scheduling
  • Invoices & payments
  • Reviews & follow-up
  • Paperwork & admin

01 Missed calls & messages

Missing calls while your hands are full?

Every missed call gets an instant text back, so the customer isn’t already dialing the next guy.

Customers calling to ask where you are?

An “on my way” text fires when you leave the last job. The phone stops ringing.

Answering the same five questions on email and Facebook?

Common questions get answered instantly, in your words, on every channel you use.

Web form leads sitting until you’re off the truck?

New leads get a real reply in under a minute, before they’ve called anyone else.

02 Quotes & scheduling

Send a quote and never hear back?

Follow-ups go out on their own at day 2, 5, and 10 until you get a yes or a no.

Booking jobs by playing phone tag?

Customers pick a slot, it lands on your calendar, everyone gets a confirmation.

Calling to confirm tomorrow’s appointments?

Reminders send themselves at 24 hours and again an hour out.

No-shows just disappear?

A missed appointment triggers a rebooking text the same day, while you’re still on their mind.

Building tomorrow’s schedule by hand every night?

New jobs drop onto the schedule as they’re booked, and the crew gets the update.

03 Invoices & payments

Writing invoices at ten at night?

The invoice builds and sends itself the moment a job is marked complete.

Chasing people for money you already earned?

Overdue invoices get polite reminders on a schedule until they pay. You never make the call.

Matching payments to invoices by hand?

Payments sync and match automatically, so the books stay current without you touching a keyboard.

Card declines you find out about a month later?

Failed payments retry on their own and flag you the same day if they still don’t clear.

04 Reviews & follow-up

Great work, nothing to show for it on Google?

A review request goes out automatically the moment a job is marked complete.

Customers from two years ago you’ve never called again?

Seasonal check-ins and service reminders go out without you remembering to send them.

05 Paperwork & admin

Still shoeboxing receipts until tax season?

Snap a photo from the truck. It’s read, categorized, tagged to the job, and logged in QuickBooks before you’re back in the driver’s seat.

Job photos stuck on your phone?

Photos from the field file themselves into the right job folder the day you take them.

Entering the same job into two different systems?

Enter it once. Everything downstream updates itself, with nothing retyped.

Downloading files from one system just to upload them into another?

Files move between your systems on their own, in the background, while you work.

Digging through QuickBooks to see how you did?

A plain-english summary of the month lands in your inbox on the first.

↓ Pricing one-pager

Straight answers.

Do I have to buy new software?

+

No. You keep QuickBooks, Jobber, Housecall Pro, whatever you already pay for — in your name, on your accounts. I connect to what’s already there.

Do I have to learn anything?

+

No. It runs behind the tools you already use. If it’s working, you shouldn’t notice it at all — that’s the whole point.

What happens when it breaks at six in the morning?

+

It’s monitored around the clock. I usually know before you do, and fixing it is included in your monthly plan, not billed as an extra.

How do I know it’s actually working?

+

Every workflow is monitored, and you get a plain-english summary of what ran and what it caught. If something stops, I hear about it, not you.

Can I start with just one thing?

+

You should. Pick the one that annoys you most, let it run for a month, and add more once you trust it. Most people do it that way.

Why is there a monthly plan at all?

+

Because software nobody watches quietly stops working. The monthly plan is hosting, monitoring, and repairs — it’s what keeps the thing alive after launch.
